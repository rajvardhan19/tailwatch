# Flight ingestion and visits

`FlightPage` is the normalized adapter boundary. A completed leg retains source identity, actual/inferred timing, airport identities, availability and evidence. Unresolved aircraft/airport IDs remain null. Source snapshots require stable watermark and cadence across pages. `collect_flights` hashes each raw response, authorizes and reserves requests/cost before every retry/page, rejects pagination cycles, deduplicates identical legs and quarantines overlapping/conflicting identities. An incomplete run cannot produce zero-activity evidence. Replay saved pages to resume computation; repeated paid requests require a durable budget ledger before enabling a paid adapter.

`append_flight_batch` stores complete normalized batches through existing atomic evidence publication, with retained raw-page dependencies. Incomplete/quarantined batches remain diagnostic artifacts, not published feature inputs. Callers must construct facts whose availability includes the database publication boundary. `ADSB.lol` raw-state capture is a separate bounded adapter: it does not fabricate normalized completed legs from isolated positions. Current automated leg processing uses synthetic/local normalized pages; trace coverage and airport ground-transition validation are still needed for the live provider.

`completed_visits` requires a known arrival and the next observed departure of that same airframe at the same airport, plus uninterrupted complete coverage between them. Partial/outage coverage prevents dwell attribution. A future departure cannot complete an earlier view. Returned objects describe aircraft dwell, not people or meetings. Upstream C11 control approval, registry freshness and the public-company universe must be enforced when mapping airframes to visitor companies.

Host attribution consumes C12's deduplicated company distances. Own-company, reviewed related-company exclusions, home-base and maintenance alternatives are explicit. Weights use exp(-distance/rho)/(background+sum), with a nonzero background; defaults are rho=25 km and background=1. Evidence-backed event overlaps apply a single 0.2 multiplier regardless of match count. Missing event coverage remains explicit. These are research heuristics, not meeting probabilities.
